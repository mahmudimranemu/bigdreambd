A paper house built on mill relationships

Big Dream Corporation is an importer, indentor and stockist of paper and board based in Banasree, Dhaka. We work directly with mills in Italy, South Africa, Germany, Korea, China, Indonesia and India, so our customers get genuine mill grades at mill-level prices.

Printers come to us for art card and coated paper, packaging houses for FBB, duplex and kraft liners, and corporate buyers for writing paper, stickers and fancy stock for their stationery and gifts. Whatever the job, we help you pick the right grade and GSM and get it to your floor on time.

How you can buy from us

Indent

Direct import from the mill against your LC. Best for large, planned orders: you get mill pricing, the exact grade and GSM you need, and we handle the paperwork and follow-up from order to port.

Ready stock

Popular grades held in our Dhaka warehouse, available in sheets or reels for quick delivery. Buy the quantity you need for this job without waiting on a shipment.

Paper consultancy

Not sure which board or GSM fits your packaging, book or print job? We advise on grade selection, specifications and alternatives that cut cost without losing quality on press.

Our products

Every grade below is available on indent, and many are in stock in Dhaka. Ask us for GSM ranges and sheet or reel sizes.

  • Art card

    Gloss and matt coated board for catalogues, covers, cards and premium print.

  • Art paper

    Coated paper with sharp colour for brochures, magazines and posters.

  • Folding box board (FBB) / Swedish board

    Bright, stiff board for pharma, cosmetics and food cartons.

  • Brief card / solid card

    Solid bleached board for folders, tags and high-end packaging.

  • Duplex board

    Grey back and white back grades for everyday cartons and boxes.

  • Virgin kraft liner

    Strong, long-fibre liner for export cartons and heavy-duty boxes.

  • Semi virgin liner

    Balanced strength and price for corrugated packaging.

  • White top kraft liner

    Printable white surface on a kraft base for retail-ready boxes.

  • Test liner

    Recycled liner for cost-effective corrugated board.

  • Medium paper

    Fluting medium for corrugated board production.

  • SCF kraft paper

    Sack and carry-bag kraft with high tear strength.

  • Self-adhesive sticker paper

    Label stock for product labels, barcodes and stickers.

  • Tissue paper

    Soft tissue grades for wrapping, napkins and converting.

  • Writing & printing paper

    Offset and copier paper for books, forms and office use.

  • Fancy special paper

    Textured, metallic and coloured stock for invitations, gifts and stationery.
